## AEW Full Gear 2026: Date, Start Time, How to Watch, Match Card and Everything You Need to Know

Phoenix becomes the setting for AEW’s next [Full Gear](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/aew-event/aew-full-gear-2026) chapter on Saturday 14th November, as the annual pay-per-view heads to Mortgage Matchup Center.

[WrestleVerse](https://wrestleverse.app/) looks ahead to the venue, viewing options and booking possibilities surrounding Full Gear 2026.

## Start Time and Global Viewing Options

Full Gear 2026 is set to kick off with the Zero Hour pre-show at 4pm Pacific Time. The pay-per-view itself starts at 5pm Pacific Time, which is equivalent to the following times around the world:
* 🇺🇸 New York - 8pm
* 🇧🇷 São Paulo - 10pm
* 🇬🇧 London - 1am (the start of Sunday 15th November)
* 🇫🇷 Paris - 2am (15th November)
* 🇦🇪 Dubai - 6am (15th November)
* 🇯🇵 Tokyo - 9am (15th November)

UK viewers should check [MyAEW](https://myaew.com/) for Full Gear access details once event-specific ordering information is published, whilst viewers in the United States should check [HBO Max](https://www.hbomax.com/aew) for availability and purchase requirements. HBO Max pay-per-view events require both a subscription and a separate event purchase.

AEW’s current pay-per-view guidance also lists additional providers for some events and territories so availability should be checked against the specific Full Gear listing before purchasing.

## Full Gear 2026 Venue and Location

Full Gear will take place at Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix, Arizona, on Saturday 14th November.

## Match Card and Preview

The full card ahead of this Saturday:
* AEW Women's World Tag Team Championship Match - The Brawling Birds (Jamie Hayter & Alex Windsor) vs. Wrathlander (Harley Cameron & Kris Statlander)
* AEW World Team Team Championship Match - The Young Bucks (Matt Jackson & Nick Jackson) vs. Davis and Doyle (Mark Davis & Jake Doyle)
* AEW World Trios Championship Texas Deathmatch - "Hangman" Adam Page & Brodido (Brody King & Bandido) vs. Swerve Strickland & The New Level (Austin Creed & Kofi)
* AEW World Championship Match - Will Ospreay vs. Kyle Fletcher
* Anarchy in the Arena Match - Death Riders (Jon Moxley, Daniel Garcia, Gabe Kidd & Wheeler Yuta) & The Conglomeration (Orange Cassidy, Kyle O'Reilly & Roderick Strong) vs. The Cut (Jay White, Ace Austin, Austin Gunn, Clark Connors, Colten Gunn, David Finlay & Juice Robinson)
* AEW Women's World Championship Match - Mercedes Moné vs. Marina Shafir
* AEW International Championship Match - Kazuchika Okada vs. Adam Copeland

## Last Year at Full Gear

Full Gear 2025 delivered a major championship change when Samoa Joe defeated ‘Hangman’ Adam Page in a Steel Cage match to become a two-time AEW World Champion. HOOK’s interference proved decisive, revealing his continued allegiance to The Opps.

Other notable moments included:
* Kyle O'Reilly defeating Jon Moxley
* PAC defeating Darby Allin by submission
* Swerve Strickland returning as the event ended

From a booking perspective, the main event did more than change the champion. It used an allegiance reveal to influence the result and gave the audience another development to follow beyond the title match.

That is the challenge for the 2026 edition: delivering a memorable night whilst leaving the next chapter ready to unfold.
