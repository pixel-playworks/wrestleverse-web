# The February Update

WrestleVerse 26.2 is now live on the App Store for iOS and iPadOS! The headline feature in this month's update is a brand new tool we've been wanting to build for a while: the _Auto Booker_.

# Auto Booker v1

The _Auto Booker_ serves 2 purposes:
* allowing the player to quickly put together a card
* simulating the shows of the rival promotions

Tap the new 'Auto' button on any show and the engine fills the remaining slots on your card with sensible matchups drawn from your roster, so you can get to the action faster.

# Everything Else

Here's every other little detail that's new in this update:
* Updated text in various places of the app to be more concise
* Slightly faster generation speed for the Commentary Engine
* Added a button to dismiss the keyboard when writing a custom promo
