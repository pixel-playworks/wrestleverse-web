## WWE Crown Jewel 2026: Date, Start Time, How to Watch, Match Card and Everything You Need to Know

Champions collide and fantasy-booking debates take centre stage as WWE's [Crown Jewel](https://www.wwe.com/shows/wwe-crown-jewel) returns to Riyadh on Saturday 7th November for an action-packed blockbuster show.
The biggest names from [RAW](https://www.wwe.com/shows/raw) and [SmackDown](https://www.wwe.com/shows/smackdown) will return to the Kingdom as part of Riyadh Season.
With Crown Jewel back in Saudi Arabia for the first time since November 2024, it promises to be another unforgettable night of action as a new set of Crown Jewel champions are crowned.
Crown Jewel will be the third WWE Premium Live Event in Saudi Arabia in 2026, ahead of WrestleMania 43 heading to the Middle East in April 2027, the first WrestleMania to be held outside North America.

## Start time and global viewing options

Wrestling fans in the United Kingdom, Canada, Japan, Mexico, and the vast majority of countries around the world can watch Crown Jewel live on [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/gb/browse/genre/81921064), which is part of WWE's international rights agreement.
Crown Jewel will also be livestreamed on ESPN's direct-to-consumer service for fans in the United States.
The 2026 Crown Jewel will start at 7pm (AST), which is 4pm (GMT).
Start times around the world:
* London: 4pm
* Paris: 5pm
* Dubai: 8pm
* Tokyo: 1am the following day
* São Paulo: 1pm
* New York: 11am
* Los Angeles: 8am

## Crown Jewel 2026 venue and location

The 2026 Crown Jewel will take place at Riyadh Season Stadium, a purpose-built temporary outdoor venue located in the King Abdullah Financial District.

## Match Card preview

So far, only the two champion-versus-champion matches have been announced. RAW's World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns will face his former Honorary Uce and Bloodline member, SmackDown's Undisputed WWE Champion, Sami Zayn, for the WWE Crown Jewel Championship. Meanwhile newly crowned RAW's Women's World Champion Liv Morgan will take on SmackDown's WWE Women's Champion Chelsea Green for the WWE Women's Crown Jewel Championship.
For the fantasy bookers watching, that creates an intriguing challenge, how do you choose a winner when both competitors already sit at the top of their respective divisions?
Would you give one champion a decisive victory or build a finish that keeps both looking strong? And what would you book next for the wrestler who falls short?
As the card takes shape, there will be plenty to debate, not just who wins, but how each match fits into the bigger picture.

## Last year at Crown Jewel

Last year's Crown Jewel, held in Perth, Western Australia, in October 2025, saw Seth 'Freakin' Rollins defeat Cody Rhodes in the Men's Crown Jewel Championship match, whilst Stephanie Vaquer reigned supreme as Women's Crown Jewel Champion by overcoming Tiffany Stratton.
To refresh your memory of the 2025 edition, here are the results:
* Bronson Reed, with Paul Heyman, defeated Roman Reigns by pinfall - Australian Street Fight
* Stephanie Vaquer, RAW's Women's World Champion, defeated Tiffany Stratton, SmackDown's WWE Women's Champion, by pinfall - Singles match for the 2025 WWE Women's Crown Jewel Championship
* John Cena defeated AJ Styles by pinfall - Singles match
* IYO SKY and Rhea Ripley defeated The Kabuki Warriors, Asuka and Kairi Sane, by pinfall - Tag team match
* Seth Rollins, RAW's World Heavyweight Champion, defeated Cody Rhodes, SmackDown’s Undisputed WWE Champion, by pinfall - Singles match for the 2025 WWE Crown Jewel Championship
The card offers plenty of inspiration for anyone who enjoys planning a wrestling show: champion-versus-champion encounters, a marquee singles match, tag team action and a stipulation bout.
