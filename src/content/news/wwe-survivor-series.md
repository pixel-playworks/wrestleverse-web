## WWE Survior Series 2026: Date, Start Time, How to Watch, Match Card and Everything You Need to Know

[WWE's Survivor Series: WarGames](https://www.wwe.com/shows/survivor-series-wargames) heads to Houston on Saturday 28th November, setting the stage for an explosive night as one of wrestling's most celebrated events reaches its landmark 40th edition.

The biggest names from [RAW](https://www.wwe.com/shows/raw) and [SmackDown](https://www.wwe.com/shows/smackdown) will head to Texas for one of the most anticipated events on the WWE calendar including WWE legends John Cena and The Rock.

With Survivor Series back in Houston for the first time since 2017, it promises to be another unforgettable night of action as rivalries take centre stage.

The 2026 show will mark the 40th annual Survivor Series, with [Daikin Park](https://www.mlb.com/astros/ballpark) providing the setting for this landmark edition.

Survivor Series offers plenty to get excited about. [WrestleVerse](https://wrestleverse.app/) guides you through all you need to know ahead of the 2026 event.

## Start time and global viewing options

Wrestling fans in the United Kingdom, Canada, Japan, Mexico, and the vast majority of countries around the world can watch Survivor Series live on [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/gb/browse/genre/81921064), which is part of WWE's international rights agreement.

Survivor Series will also be livestreamed on ESPN's direct-to-consumer service for fans in the United States. Access is available through the ESPN Unlimited plan.

The show is currently listed to begin at 6pm Eastern Time on Saturday 28th November. Start times around the world:

* 🇬🇧 London - 11pm
* 🇫🇷 Paris - Midnight (the following day)
* 🇦🇪 Dubai - 3am (the following day)
* 🇯🇵 Tokyo - 8am (the following day)
* 🇧🇷 São Paulo - 8pm
* 🇺🇸 New York - 6pm
* 🇺🇸 Los Angeles - 3pm

## Survivor Series 2026 venue and location

The 2026 Survivor Series will be held at Daikin Park, Houston, Texas, the home of Major League Baseball's Houston Astros.

## Match Card preview

The following matches and appearances have been announced for Survivor Series 2026, with further updates expected ahead of the event:

* The Rock returns to WWE to celebrate the 30th anniversary of his debut
* John Cena will also announce more participants for the John Cena Classic
* Women's WarGames Match: Charlotte Flair, Stephanie Vaquer, Bayley, Alexa Bliss & Tatum Paxley vs Jade Cargill, Michin, Jacy Jayne, Fallon Henley & Lainey Reid
* Women's World Championship Match: Liv Morgan (c) vs Becky Lynch
* 5-on-5 Survivor Series Match: Team USA (Cody Rhodes, CM Punk, Je'Von Evans, LA Knight & Seth Rollins) vs Team Mexico (Rey Mysterio, Penta, Rey Fenix, Dragon Lee & El Grande Americano)
* Men's WarGames Match: The Bloodline (Roman Reigns, Jacob Fatu, Solo Sikoa & The Usos) vs The 946 (Royce Keys, Carmelo Hayes, Cruz Montana & OTM)

## Last year at Survivor Series

Last year's Survivor Series saw Brock Lesnar, Drew McIntyre, Logan Paul, Bron Breakker & Bronson Reed triumph in the Men's WarGames match, whilst Rhea Ripley, AJ Lee, Charlotte Flair, IYO SKY & Alexa Bliss secured victory in the Women's WarGames match.

To refresh your memory of the 2025 edition, here are the results:

* Rhea Ripley, AJ Lee, Charlotte Flair, IYO SKY & Alexa Bliss defeated Becky Lynch, Asuka, Kairi Sane, Nia Jax & Lash Legend by submission - Women's WarGames match
* Dominik Mysterio defeated John Cena by pinfall - Singles match for the Intercontinental Championship
* Stephanie Vaquer defeated Nikki Bella by pinfall - Singles match for the Women's World Championship
* Brock Lesnar, Drew McIntyre, Logan Paul, Bron Breakker & Bronson Reed defeated Roman Reigns, Cody Rhodes, CM Punk, Jimmy Uso & Jey Uso by pinfall - Men's WarGames match
